What changed from the nav logo

· Crescent moved to center and enlarged ~3× — fills the icon canvas
· Star moved to upper right (composes against crescent's curve) and given a glow
· Added small accent star bottom-left for visual rhythm
· Solid violet-to-black gradient background — no transparency, full bleed
· Increased moon contrast with subtle vertical gradient and stroke
· Added ornate gold rings and corner sigils for "tarot" identity at larger sizes
· Gold (#f4d17a) on deep violet (#170833) — passes WCAG large-element contrast comfortably
